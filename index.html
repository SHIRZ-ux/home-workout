5-Day Home Workout

No equipment, just your body and a backpack. About 30 minutes a day. Days 6 and 7 are rest.

Rules

  • Start easy: 2 sets in weeks 1–2, then 3 sets.
  • Form first: slow and clean beats fast and sloppy.
  • Backpack: start with a few books, add one every week or two.
  • Progress: add 1–2 reps a week and write your numbers down.
  • Stop if a joint hurts. Muscle tiredness is normal, sharp pain is not.
  • Eat and sleep: 8–9 hours. You’re still growing.

Improving, not proving.
